## 2. Accepted evidence

AWS shows model-specific MU rates. Use an account-manager quote for those rates. [observed] [AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/prov-throughput.html)
Google GSUs use model-specific accounting and rolling windows. [observed] [Google](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/provisioned-throughput/use-provisioned-throughput)
Azure uses workload-specific PTUs. Billing Reservations do not show deployment capacity as guaranteed. [observed] [Azure latency](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/openai/how-to/latency) · [Azure onboarding](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/openai/how-to/provisioned-throughput-onboarding)
Google checks throughput against declared p99 latency limits. [observed] [Benchmarking](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/ai-ml/accelerator-performance-benchmarking)

## Gaps
