## 2. Accepted evidence

Cerebras shows per-user output speed, not rack throughput. [observed] [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/llama-405b-inference)
Together shows a long-context workload, unequal GPU allocations, and inconsistent TPM units. [observed] [Together](https://www.together.ai/blog/coding-agent-benchmarks)
The checked GB300 article uses total-token economics and interpolated iso-interactivity values. Do not use its 27 tok/s point as directly measured output-only capacity. [observed + inference] [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com/blog/gb300-nvl72-vs-gb200-nvl72-dsv4-pro-vllm-fp4)
RunPod shows zero-worker readiness separately from warm serving. [observed] [RunPod](https://docs.runpod.io/serverless/overview)
