[calculated] Complete two-hour observation of 72 GPUs means 144 GPU-hours.
Zero events set a 95% Poisson upper rate bound of approximately `0.0208 events/GPU-hour`.
The calculation uses `-ln(0.05)/exposure`.
[NIST probability formula](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366j.htm).

[inference] Constant rate, independence, homogeneous exposure, and complete detection are necessary assumptions.
The calculation does not show actual GB300 reliability.
