Research date: 2026-10-11.

Question: What GPU quality does ClusterMAX actually test?
Dependencies: None. State: done.

[observed] ClusterMAX 3.0 shows GEMM, memory paths, numerical correctness, and eight-hour mixed burn-in.
It also shows DCGM injection, synthetic logs, and a disruptive PCIe bridge reset.
Performance and reliability scripts remain internal.
[Source](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/clustermax-30-the-industry-standard).

[observed] Reliability criteria name ECC, XIDs/SXIDs, NVLink, thermal, power, PCIe, and version consistency.
[Source](https://www.clustermax.ai/criteria/reliability).

[observed] The public audit marks some monitoring coverage as partial.
A requirement label does not show that an active collector measures it.
[Source](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/ClusterMAX/blob/1492ac5e4ac992ae436f062cc51a340d61672ca3/cmax/scripts/1-audit/criteria-checks.json).

[inference] Injection tests monitoring response, not physical defect detection.
A two-hour screen cannot claim eight-hour burn-in equivalence.

## Gaps

TinyMeg2 appears in historical top-provider practice, not verified current 3.0/GB300 use.
Public evidence does not show complete GPU-only scoring, thresholds, or executable reliability tests.
