## Accepted brief

Full child evidence: [large-companies.md](../large-companies.md).
Question: What do company practices show about training failure detection and containment?
Acceptance: Check telemetry, screening, diagnosis, restart, and transfer limits.

MegaScale uses heartbeats, rank timing, traffic metrics, diagnostics, and node exclusion.
An event sequence can show a loader stall on another rank before a collective timeout.
Meta shows static, transient, and silent faults as separate classes.
A corruption check can show silent in-range gradient corruption after weeks of delay.
Megatron Bridge shows constrained workload-side resiliency mechanisms.
The Meta blog shows >95%; the 405B paper shows >90%.

## Limits

Do not use published rates and performance as direct GB300 NVL72 results.
Two hours do not show historical suspect-node detection.
Loss-only monitoring does not show silent-error absence.
